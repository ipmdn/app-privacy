NDTCertify — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

In short: NDTCertify is a local-first app. Your certificate data, photos, and master data are stored on your device and, if you choose to enable it, in your own iCloud account. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your data, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers of any kind.

Who we are

NDTCertify is developed by Matteo Del Negro. For any privacy question, contact us at ipmdn@icloud.com.

Data storage and where it lives

  • On-device database. All certificates, inspection records, photos, and master data (customers, operators, equipment, standard methods, production orders, etc.) are stored in a local database on your iPad. We never receive a copy of this data.
  • Optional iCloud backup. If you enable "iCloud Backup" in Settings, your local database and photo attachments are copied into your personal iCloud Drive container, using Apple's iCloud infrastructure and your own Apple ID. This is single-user, single-device-at-a-time file storage — we have no access to this data; it is encrypted and managed entirely by Apple under your Apple ID.

Face ID / Touch ID

NDTCertify uses Face ID or Touch ID, when available, to confirm you are the device owner before two specific irreversible actions:

  • Erasing the local database.
  • Transferring database ownership when this database was last used on another device.

Biometric authentication is handled entirely by iOS on your device (via Apple's LocalAuthentication framework). NDTCertify never receives, stores, or transmits your biometric data — the app only receives a yes/no confirmation from the operating system.

Camera and Photo Library

NDTCertify requests camera access to let inspectors attach photographic evidence to a certificate directly from an inspection. Photo library access is requested only as a fallback when a camera is unavailable on the device. Photos you capture or attach are stored in the app's local (and optionally iCloud) database described above — they are not uploaded anywhere else.

What we do not do

  • We do not run analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not operate a server that stores your certificates or inspection data in this version of the app.

Required-reason API usage

As disclosed in the app's privacy manifest, NDTCertify uses two categories of iOS "required reason" APIs strictly for on-device functionality:

  • UserDefaults — to store your own app preferences (e.g. connection mode, PDF export folder settings) locally on your device. This data is never read by, or shared with, any other app.
  • File timestamps — to show you when your iCloud backup last synced, and to detect whether an iCloud file has finished downloading to your device.

Future cloud sync (Supabase)

A future version of this app may offer an optional multi-device/multi-user sync mode. That capability is not enabled in this version of the app. This policy will be updated, and you will be asked for consent, before any such feature collects or transmits your data to a server.

Children's privacy

NDTCertify is a professional tool for certified NDT (Non-Destructive Testing) inspectors and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, make that clear the next time you open the app.
